A benefit concert for Belev Echad

Belev Echad is an international nonprofit helping wounded IDF soldiers get their lives back, with physical, medical, legal, emotional and financial support. Since October 7 the need has grown dramatically, and Belev Echad stands with every soldier and their family, every step of the way. Every ticket helps.

Questions

Is it children friendly?

Yes. It's a fun family night for all ages. No soldiers will be speaking at the event.

Is it tax deductible?

Anything you give above the ticket price is tax deductible. Receipts come from Chabad 101.

Where do I park?

Free parking on the street, plus hundreds of free spots across the street at Harbor Island Park.

Where is it?

Chabad 101, 101 Mamaroneck Avenue, Mamaroneck, New York. The lighting is outside at the corner of Boston Post Road and Mamaroneck Avenue, and the concert is inside.

What time should we come?

Be there by 7:00 pm for the outdoor menorah lighting. The concert starts at 7:30 pm.

Will there be security?

Yes. Mamaroneck Police and private security will be at the event.

Do I need to print my ticket?

You can bring a printed ticket or show it on your phone.

Where does the money go?

The night supports Belev Echad and its work with wounded IDF veterans and their families.
